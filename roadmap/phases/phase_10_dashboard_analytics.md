# Phase 10 — Dashboard & Personal Analytics
**Goal:** Dashboard metrics from actual personal data.

## Target metrics
total titles, watched, unwatched, watching, completed, dropped, favorites,
total watch hours, movies watched, series completed, anime completed,
countries explored, languages explored, watch streak, monthly watch count,
annual watch count, average personal rating.

## Constraint
Metrics must be derived from canonical/user data. Do not store redundant
analytics unless justified.
