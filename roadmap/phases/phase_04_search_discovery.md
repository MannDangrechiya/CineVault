# Phase 4 — Search & Discovery
**Goal:** High-quality catalog search.

## Support
title, original title, aliases, transliteration, multilingual titles, genres,
countries, languages, years, content type, people, franchises, themes.

## Required multilingual test cases
- Parasite / 기생충 / Gisaengchung
- Your Name / 君の名は。/ Kimi no Na wa

## Constraint
Search stays PostgreSQL-first unless measurement proves a dedicated search
engine is required. Do not introduce a search engine prematurely.
